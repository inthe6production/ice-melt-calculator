How the math works

Base rate used: about ½ cup (≈4 oz) per square yard per application — OxyChem’s published calcium-chloride guidance is 4 oz per sq yd (28 lb per 1,000 sq ft), and Morton’s bag directions use ½ cup per sq yd. Blends/salt need a little more, calcium chloride a little less. Always start light — you can add more; over-applying damages concrete and plants and does not melt faster.

Sources: OxyChem Calcium Chloride “Melting Ice — Sidewalks” (oxycalciumchloride.com/use-responsibly/melting-ice-sidewalks/); Morton Action Melt directions via directionsforme.org.
